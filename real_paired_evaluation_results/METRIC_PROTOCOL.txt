Models: Stage 4 best checkpoints for seeds 17, 43, and 73.
Preprocessing: radiometric.
Evaluation resolution: 512x512 for every image.
All matched image pairs were scored.
Only ten prediction images per model and dataset were saved.
CIEDE2000 was computed after clipping RGB values to [0,1], converting RGB to Lab with scikit-image, and averaging the per-pixel CIEDE2000 values. Lower DeltaE00 is better.
I-HAZE and NH-HAZE are the attached five-pair test subsets.
